Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our courses and admission process.

What are the admission criteria for ESAT?

expand_more

Admission is based on past academic performance and an introductory counseling session. For certain advanced batches, students may be required to take our Elementary Science Aptitude Test (ESAT).

Do you provide study materials?

expand_more

Yes, comprehensive and updated study material is provided for all courses, covering board exams as well as competitive exams like JEE, NEET, and NDA.

What is the student-to-teacher ratio?

expand_more

We believe in personal mentorship and maintain a small batch size, typically not exceeding 30 students per batch, ensuring every student gets individual attention.

Are there demo classes available?

expand_more

Demo classes and counselling slots are available by appointment. Call or message us on WhatsApp to confirm the next available time.
